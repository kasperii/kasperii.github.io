Keywords: Type design, Display typeface, Poster design

Urbscape is a display typeface I designed in 2019 to explore the craft of making custom typefaces. It was made for poster design, so it works best large. A fair warning: the kerning is uneven, so check the spacing by eye.

Upper and lower case are both capitals: they give two alternative versions of each uppercase letter, so you can mix them for flexibility.

It's free to use and share under the SIL Open Font License.
