Conway's Game of Life on a 50 × 50 board that wraps around at the edges. The rules are not intuitive and neither are the buttons, but click around and see what happens: click cells to bring them to life, the blue button plays and pauses, the pink one resets. Cells that have lived leave a trail that slowly fades. Every block you bring to life counts as cleared, and the lifespan grows for as long as the board keeps moving: the more that moves, the faster it grows.
