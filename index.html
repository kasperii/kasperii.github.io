Human-Centred Technology, Research & Design
User Research @ Spotify

This is my humble webpage. I work where people meet technology — investigating how we live with it, designing how it could be, and building the prototypes in between. I hold a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from Stockholm University, where I researched self-tracking, sleep and agency alongside gaze interaction, social robots and virtual reality, with research visits to Copenhagen and Tokyo University. Before that I studied Computer Science and Media Technology at KTH and designed concepts, brands and animations at Netlight. Today I do user research at Spotify, and still take on the occasional design project — most recently design and brand work for the startup altego AI and YC-backed Scape.
