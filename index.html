User Research @ Spotify

This is my humble webpage. I am a design-driven developer doing research in the gap between technology, sociology and design, and I like to be part of the whole process, from sketching concepts to tinkering with the interaction. I hold a PhD in Human-Computer Interaction from Stockholm University, where I researched self-tracking, sleep and agency alongside gaze interaction, social robots and virtual reality, with research visits to Copenhagen and Tokyo University. Before that I studied Computer Science and Media Technology at KTH and designed concepts, brands and animations at Netlight. Today I do user research at Spotify, and still take on the occasional design project, most recently design and brand work for the startup altego AI and YC-backed Scape.
